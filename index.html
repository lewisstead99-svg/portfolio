01 — Intro

Isn't just a tray.

Turned from a single blank of American black walnut and finished by hand in hardwax oil. Holm gives your keys, your watch and the coins in your pocket one place to land, and makes the simplest moment of the day feel considered.

No lacquer to chip. No felt to peel. A shallow pocket that stops things rolling and a soft lip that stops them leaving. You will notice Holm for a week. After that it is simply where things go.
