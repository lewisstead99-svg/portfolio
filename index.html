A shallow pocket, turned fourteen millimetres deep. Keys settle, coins stop rolling, a watch lies flat. Nothing tips out when you pick it up.

Four hundred and ten grams of solid walnut on a turned foot ring. It does not slide, rock or mark the desk. Put it down once and it stays where it lands.

Hardwax oil, not lacquer. The finish is in the timber, not on it, so nothing chips or peels. Every year the walnut darkens a shade, and a scratch simply becomes grain.
